## 3. Key Business Insights

### A. Revenue by Region

- South region generated the highest revenue of approximately 2.9M.
- North region generated approximately 2.8M.
- West region contributed approximately 2.1M.
- East region generated approximately 1.8M.
- Central region generated approximately 1.5M.

**Insight:** South and North are the leading revenue-generating regions, while Central has the lowest revenue contribution.

**Recommendation:** Focus on improving sales and marketing strategies in the Central and East regions.

### B. Revenue by Category

- Furniture generated approximately 5.1M.
- Electronics generated approximately 4.6M.
- Accessories generated approximately 1.1M.
- Office Supplies generated approximately 0.4M.

**Insight:** Furniture is the highest revenue-generating category, followed by Electronics.

**Recommendation:** Maintain strong inventory and marketing support for Furniture and Electronics while exploring opportunities to improve the performance of Office Supplies.

### C. Revenue by Sales Channel

- Online sales contributed approximately 6.1M.
- Retail sales contributed approximately 3.3M.
- Distributor sales contributed approximately 1.8M.

**Insight:** Online is the strongest sales channel and contributes the largest share of total revenue.

**Recommendation:** Strengthen online sales strategies and improve digital customer engagement.

### D. Monthly Revenue Trend

The monthly revenue analysis shows variations throughout 2025.

- February revenue was approximately 1.05M.
- June recorded approximately 1.14M.
- July generated approximately 1.10M.
- September generated approximately 1.06M.
- May recorded the lowest revenue at approximately 0.72M.

**Insight:** June recorded the highest monthly revenue, while May recorded the lowest.

**Recommendation:** Analyze the reasons behind monthly fluctuations and plan promotional campaigns during lower-performing months.

### E. Top 10 Products by Revenue

The highest revenue-generating products include:

1. Monitor – 3.2M
2. Study Table – 2.2M
3. Office Chair – 1.8M
4. Bookshelf – 1.1M
5. Headphones – 0.6M
6. Webcam – 0.5M
7. Keyboard – 0.5M
8. Laptop Bag – 0.4M
9. USB Hub – 0.3M
10. Wireless Mouse – 0.3M

**Insight:** Monitor, Study Table, and Office Chair are the leading products by revenue.

**Recommendation:** Focus on maintaining the availability of high-performing products and explore cross-selling opportunities.
